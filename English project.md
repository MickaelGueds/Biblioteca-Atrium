# Lesson 1: How Can We Grow Together?

## Part 1: Talk About Yourself

My name is Mickael. I am a software engineering student and I work with technology.

I am a calm person, but I am also very focused when I have a goal. I like to learn new things, but sometimes I am a little impatient when I don’t understand something fast. I think this is something I need to improve.

I am responsible with my work and my studies. I know that discipline is important, because we don’t grow only when we are motivated. We grow when we continue doing the right things, even in difficult days.

My family is important to me, and my friends are important too. I like to spend time with people who make me feel good and help me see life in a better way.

## Part 2: Describe Your Routine

My routine is simple, but busy.

On weekdays, I usually work, study, and practice programming. I spend many hours in front of the computer. Sometimes I am tired, but I try to keep my discipline because I know my future depends of my choices today.

I study English too, because I want to improve my communication. I know I make mistakes, but I think mistakes are part of learning.

On the weekends, my routine is usually quiet. I like to rest, play games, and sometimes go out to talk with my friends. I don’t always like very noisy places. Sometimes I prefer stay at home and relax.

I like to play, study, talk with friends, and learn about technology. I don’t like to waste time, but I also understand that rest is necessary.

---

## Part 3: Analyze Your Skills

I think I am good at solving problems. When something is difficult, I try to understand it step by step.

I am also good at learning alone. I like to search, test, make mistakes, and try again. This help me grow.

But I need help with some things. I need to improve my English, my speaking, and sometimes my patience. I also need to improve how I explain my ideas to other people.

I can help people with technology, programming, and organization. I can also help when someone needs to be calm to understand a problem.

But I believe I can learn a lot from other people too. Some people are better at communication. Some people are better at creativity. Some people are better at leadership. When we share this, everyone grow together.

---

## Part 4: Working as a Team

Yes, it is good to work in a team.

For me, teamwork is not only about doing a task together. It is about discipline, responsibility, and empathy.

Discipline is important because a team needs people who do their part. If one person don’t do the work, the whole team can have problems.

Responsibility is important because people need to trust each other. When I say I will do something, I need to try my best to do it.

Empathy is important because everybody has difficult days. Sometimes a person needs help, patience, or just someone to listen. A good team is not a team without problems. A good team is a team that solve problems together.

We can grow together when we respect different opinions, help people with their difficulties, and accept help too.

---

## Conclusion

We can grow together with discipline, responsibility, and empathy.

Discipline helps us continue. Responsibility helps people trust us. Empathy helps us understand each other.

I think nobody grows completely alone. We need people to teach us, correct us, support us, and challenge us.

So, to grow together, we need to do our part and also care about the people around us
